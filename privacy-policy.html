Privacy Policy for Canter

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

Overview

Canter, formerly RunWalk ("the App"), is a run-walk interval timer available for iOS, watchOS, and Android. Your privacy is important to us, and we want to be transparent about our data practices.

In short: Your data stays on your device. We don't collect, transmit, or sell any of your information.

Data We Access

Location Data

The App accesses your device's GPS location only during active workouts to:

  • Track your running/walking route
  • Calculate distance traveled
  • Display your route on a map
Important:
  • Location tracking is optional and can be disabled in Settings
  • Location data is stored locally on your device only
  • We do NOT transmit your location to any servers
  • We do NOT share your location with third parties
  • Location data is only collected while the App is actively tracking a workout

Health & Fitness Data

On iOS and watchOS (Apple HealthKit)

With your explicit permission, the App may:

  • Write: Workout sessions, duration, distance, active calories burned
  • Read: Your workout history (to display past run-walk sessions)

On Android (Health Connect)

With your explicit permission, the App may:

  • Write: Exercise sessions, distance, active calories burned
  • Read: Your workout history (to display past run-walk sessions)
Important:
  • Health data is protected by Apple's HealthKit / Google's Health Connect security frameworks
  • Health data remains on your device and within the platform's health ecosystem
  • We do NOT transmit health data to any servers
  • We do NOT share health data with third parties
  • We do NOT use health data for advertising or marketing

Data We Do NOT Collect

The App:

  • Does NOT collect personal information (name, email, etc.)
  • Does NOT require an account or sign-in
  • Does NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • Does NOT contain advertisements
  • Does NOT transmit any data over the internet
  • Does NOT access your contacts, photos, or other personal data

Local Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device:

  • Workout history - Your completed workouts and routes
  • App settings - Your preferences (voice, sounds, GPS mode)
  • Custom presets - Any workout presets you create

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following platform services that operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple HealthKit (iOS/watchOS) - Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Health Connect (Android) - Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple MapKit (iOS/watchOS) - For displaying route maps
  • Google Maps (Android) - For displaying route maps

The App does not integrate with any advertising networks, analytics platforms, or other third-party data collection services.

Managing Your Data

Location Permissions

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Canter
  • Android: Settings > Apps > RunWalk > Permissions > Location

Health Permissions

  • iOS: Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Canter
  • Android: Settings > Apps > Health Connect > App permissions > RunWalk

Deleting Your Data

  • Workout history: Delete workouts within the App or from Health/Health Connect
  • All app data: Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. Health and location access requires explicit user consent.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date above. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
